FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Pure Path services, consultations, and how we support your wellbeing.

Do I need a referral?

Many appointments can be requested without a GP referral. Your practitioner will advise if further documentation is needed.

Can I visit in person or call?

Yes. You can book over the phone on (07) 5632 8124, or visit us in person at Earle Plaza — Shop G3/52 Price St, Nerang QLD 4211. We’re open Monday–Saturday 10 am–6 pm (closed Sunday).

Do you advertise medicines on this website?

No. Australian law restricts advertising prescription medicines to the public. This website only promotes our consultation services. Whether any medicine is suitable is decided only by a registered practitioner after assessment.

How does Pure Path approach clinical compliance?

Practitioners are AHPRA-registered and practise within applicable Australian laws and professional standards. Clinical decisions are individual and are never driven by website advertising.

What happens after my consultation?

Your practitioner will discuss findings and recommended next steps based on your assessment. Follow-up reviews may be arranged if appropriate. Nothing on this site promises a particular treatment or outcome.

Can I claim with Medicare?

Consultations are private appointments and are generally not bulk-billed. Some patients may be eligible for partial rebates depending on their circumstances — your practitioner can clarify what applies to you during booking or consult.

How much does a consultation cost?

Consultations start from $80. Pricing is transparent with no hidden fees. Call us or book online for current availability.
