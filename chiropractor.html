Dr Shakira Husain is a skilled chiropractor serving the Gold Coast from Pure Path at Earle Plaza, Nerang. She completed a six-year double degree pathway — Bachelor of Science and Master of Chiropractic (BSc, MChiro) — and has been supporting patients for around 15 years, including running her own clinic for over five years.

Dr Shakira specialises in addressing body pains and spinal concerns with targeted adjustments and care. Her sessions combine chiropractic assessment with bodywork modalities so patients can access integrated manual care in one private room — without open treatment bays or long wait times when you arrive on time for your booking.

Beyond spinal and joint adjusting, she is trained and certified in areas including Kahuna, Thai, and pregnancy massage, plus kinesiology (muscle testing and biomechanics). Care is practical, honest, and tailored after assessment — X-ray referral may be discussed when clinically appropriate.

Modalities offered

Spinal & joint adjusting Chiropractic assessment Dry needling Cupping Myofascial / fascia release Assisted stretching Kahuna massage Thai massage Pregnancy massage Kinesiology Whole-body care

What to expect

A private room, clear communication, and a plan discussed after assessment. Fees and any recommended follow-up are explained before you commit.

Who she works with

People seeking hands-on care for spinal and musculoskeletal concerns — from everyday aches to ongoing body pain — after individual assessment.
