Psychologist · MAPS

Miss Sarah-Jane Lodington

Building resilience, clarity and confidence through compassionate, evidence-based psychological care — at Pure Path / Mental Strength Psychology, Nerang.

MAPS member Medicare provider Telehealth available NDIS capacity building LGBTQIA+ affirming

As a dedicated psychologist, Sarah-Jane values a strong therapeutic alliance — built on trust, safety and genuineness. She offers a non-judgmental, warm and approachable space where clients can feel truly supported.

Her approach is professional, ethical, trauma-informed, neuro-affirming and collaborative. She walks alongside clients to navigate challenges, build insight, and move toward meaningful change — at a pace that feels right for each person.

Sarah-Jane brings professional experience and lived understanding of the resilience that often comes with caring roles and rebuilding after unexpected trauma. She has extensive experience working with neurodivergent individuals and takes a strengths-based, affirming approach with autism, ADHD and other neurodiverse profiles. She is proud to be an inclusive, LGBTQIA+ friendly practitioner.

Full practice details, booking and funding information are available on her site: Mental Strength Psychology.

Services overview

CBT Interpersonal therapy Trauma therapy Attachment & schema Gottman couples (Level 2 training) Psychodynamic (short-term focused) Motivational interviewing Psychoeducation General counselling EMDR (from July 2026)

Individual therapy

Evidence-based individual sessions including CBT, trauma-informed work, positive psychology approaches, and support for emotional regulation and executive functioning.

Neurodiversity & specialist support

Affirming support for autism, ADHD, intellectual disability and ABI, plus social skills and self-esteem focused work where appropriate.

Relationships & family

Couples counselling (Gottman-informed), family therapy where appropriate, boundaries, assertiveness and communication skills.

Assessments & reports

NDIS assessments and reports, diagnoses, and functional behaviour assessments — discussed after enquiry about your needs.

Funding & access

Medicare rebates may be available under a Mental Health Care Plan from your GP. Mental Strength Psychology describes a capped gap fee model (check current figures when booking) and additional subsidies for valid healthcare and pension cards. Processing fees may apply.

NDIS funded services may be available under Capacity Building — Improved Daily Living, subject to your plan and eligibility. Telehealth (including MBS telehealth where applicable), phone and internet counselling options are listed with her practice.
