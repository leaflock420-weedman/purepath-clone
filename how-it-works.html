How it works

How Pure Path telehealth & clinic care works

Our doctor-led process is simple: book, consult, and get clear next steps — whether you’re on telehealth across Australia or visiting our Nerang Gold Coast clinic.

01

Book online or call

Reserve a telehealth initial consultation online, call (07) 5632 8124, or visit Earle Plaza Nerang. Many patients start without a GP referral — your practitioner will advise if anything extra is needed.

02

Clinical consultation

Speak with an AHPRA-registered practitioner about your history, concerns, and goals. Consultations are private, confidential, and designed around you — not a one-size-fits-all script.

03

Personalised next steps

You’ll leave with clear guidance after assessment. Any care pathway is only considered when clinically appropriate for you. Follow-ups can be booked when needed.

Who Pure Path is for

People seeking a private telehealth clinic or Gold Coast in-person care for medical consultations, chiropractic, or psychology — with transparent fees from $77 for consultations and care designed around their needs.

Also at this location: chiropractor in Nerang (Dr Shakira Husain) and psychologist in Nerang (Sarah-Jane Lodington).
