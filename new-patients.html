New patients

Starting with Pure Path is simple

Whether you’re new to private telehealth or changing clinics, we make your first consultation clear, discreet, and focused on care designed for you.

What to expect as a new patient

1. Book a telehealth initial consultation online or call reception.
2. Attend your private consult with an AHPRA-registered practitioner.
3. Decide next steps together after assessment — never from a website form alone.

Consultation fees start from $77. Opening hours: Mon–Sat 10 am–6 pm at Earle Plaza, Nerang QLD 4211.
