Advertising & therapeutic goods notice

How this website is intended to align with Australian therapeutic goods advertising rules administered by the TGA.

What this website is

A public information and booking site for Pure Path clinical consultation services in Nerang, Queensland. It describes how to contact us, visit the clinic, and request an appointment with an AHPRA-registered practitioner.

What this website is not

It is not an advertisement for prescription medicines or other restricted therapeutic goods. It does not promote, offer for supply, or encourage the public to use any particular medicine (including goods that may only be accessed through lawful prescription pathways).

TGA framework (summary)

Under the Therapeutic Goods Act 1989 and related advertising rules, advertising prescription medicines to the public is generally prohibited. The TGA has published guidance on restrictions for prescription medicines and for services that prescribe or supply them. This site is designed to advertise consultations, not medicines.

Clinical decisions

Any decision about diagnosis, management, or medicines is made only by a registered health practitioner after an individual assessment. Content on this site is general information and is not personal medical advice. Outcomes are not guaranteed.

Not legal advice

This notice is provided for transparency. It is not legal advice. For formal compliance advice, consult a qualified Australian lawyer familiar with therapeutic goods advertising.

Reference: Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA)
