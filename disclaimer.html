How we work with you

We keep our public information focused on consultations and personal care — so your pathway stays individual and appropriate.

What this website is

A public information and booking site for Pure Path clinical consultation services in Nerang, Queensland. It describes how to contact us, visit the clinic, and request an appointment with an AHPRA-registered practitioner.

What we emphasise

We describe consultation services and the practitioners available at our clinic. Any specific treatment or medicine is only ever considered after a registered practitioner assesses you in person or by appointment — never as a public product offer.

Personal care first

Australian rules carefully regulate how certain medicines can be discussed in public. We respect that. Online, we talk about care designed for you, appointments, and practitioners. Clinical decisions stay between you and your registered practitioner.

Clinical decisions

Any decision about diagnosis, management, or medicines is made only by a registered health practitioner after an individual assessment. Content on this site is general information and is not personal medical advice. Outcomes are not guaranteed.

A friendly note

This page explains our approach in plain language. If you have questions about your own care, the best next step is a consultation with one of our practitioners.

Reference: Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA)
